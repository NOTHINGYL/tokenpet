## 1.0.0 — 2026-07-27

TokenPet's first public-ready source release.

### Highlights

- A Golden British desktop companion driven by local token activity.
- Sleepy, Focused, Hot, and Overdrive mood reactions with configurable signal windows.
- Eight authored Quick Play actions: Pet, Feed snack, Play cursor, Dangle toy, Toss yarn, Nap here, Wake up, and Dance.
- Direction-aware offline locomotion, fixed-anchor transparent Dance video, and synchronized audio.
- Menu bar usage summary and local console for overview, appearance, actions, and mood controls.
- Local-first storage with no TokenPet account or prompt, code, path, or session-content upload.

### Distribution

Version 1.0.0 is currently installed from source. A signed and notarized macOS installer is intentionally deferred until an Apple Developer distribution identity is available.
