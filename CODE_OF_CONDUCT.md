# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

TokenPet welcomes respectful, constructive participation. Examples of positive behavior include:

- showing empathy and patience;
- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- respecting different experience levels and viewpoints;
- protecting other people's privacy and security;
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publication of another person's private data are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where someone is representing the TokenPet community.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner through GitHub. For sensitive reports, request a private contact channel without posting details publicly. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of the community. Good-faith reports will be handled as privately as the available GitHub tools allow.
